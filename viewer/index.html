What am I looking at?

Each dot is a real neuron at its recorded soma location, or an anatomical anchor where a soma is unavailable. Lines show measured connections; they are straight links, not reconstructed axon or dendrite paths.

All mapped neurons load first. The overview limits lines for performance. Select a neuron to load its complete outgoing connection list. This is a structural viewer, not a simulation of brain activity.
